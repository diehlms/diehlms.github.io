Found myself somewhere near if not exactly in Barrow, Alaska on the state's north shore. It was during the summer, where the air was breezy and comfortable, almost unnoticeable. There is a town, or maybe was a town. All the squat tiny concrete homes were boarded up with no sign of recent habitation. I could peek in throuh the shutters and see almost nothing save for a scant couch.

Then suddenly I popped up on the back of a moving golf cart, being driven by someone presumably. I took my flip phone out and started recording a little kitty that had begun following us as we wove around the streets of barrow. The cat was able to keep up with ease. Superstition by Stevie Wonder played in the background.

I continued walking and found a coastal restaurant heavily adorned with those plastic wall leaves most commonly found on touristy cheap restaurants in Europe. Colette and I got a table easily as there was no one else there. There was a vegetarian friendly menu, which surprised us. I got orange chicken.
