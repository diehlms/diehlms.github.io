I often walk around town with headphones, listening to music or podcasts. When I take the bus to work, I often read with headphones in tuned to classical music or ambient sounds to drown out the screech of the bus brakes and other passengers. Walking around with music has been a habit since at least college, where I would sneak in a few songs between classes, or walking to/from internships and jobs across D.C. I find taking transit an especially attractive time to pop in audio, where you the passenger can star in a production of life being lived, where the unwitting audience bears tribute to the story inch music serves a fairly epic backdrop. There wasn't much impetus for the headphones when it started, and listening to something has been an automated habit which I'm sure is not unique to me.

My recent personal milestone was turning thirty. For some reason, the world soon thereafter felt louder and more oppressive. The sound of traffic was amplified. The sensation of the wind through the tree leaves was unignorably loud. I was constantly eavesdropping on conversations I couldn't bother with. Thinking became tiring, more so than normal. I was becomming overwhelmed by moving through my daily chores because it felt like my sensations were ratcheted up to a level just out of reach of sustainable. So I took the headphones off, and tucked them away until I got home.

My grocery store is a fifteen minute walk away, as is my gym. My office is about a thirty minute bike ride, a bit more by bus. Taking the headphones off and carrying on with my tasks made the tasks feel longer, and more serene. When I feel like I don't have enough of something, I can't help on concentrate on my lack of that thing. Having my ears blasted with noise every waking moment made me feel like I was missing silence and stillness and therefore I felt in deep longing. With my naked ears, my life has space to be bored in and space for my thoughts to bounce off the walls of my head again. The fifteen minute walk to the gym or store requries me to look around and take things in as they happen in front of me. There is now no decision to make on what to listen to as the world simply provides. So take the headphones ond stop listening.
