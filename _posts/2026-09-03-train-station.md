Heard a friend slash acquaintance playing a beautiful nylon string guitar in a sunny train station on a late afternoon. The train station appeared to be vacant. The archway at the end of the station's main hall opened to an arcade along a grassy patch atop the seashore. The walls of the station were a faded yellow with cracks and chips pocketmarked across most of the surface. The light was quite beautiful. The friend playing guitar does not know how to play guitar; i know this nearly for fact in my own life. But in the dream he played quite comfortably. I watched him play, a little self-concious, and walked outside to look at the waves crashing into the beach below the bluff. I was in an old library with lots of nice cornithian leather upholstery. My old colleagues and I were putting a booth on in some conference nearby where we were certain nobody would give a shit about us.
