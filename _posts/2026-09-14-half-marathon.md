At some point in the middle of a half marathon I find myself on a tree-lined boulevard. Ostensibly, this is in Austin judging
by the t-shirts and swag, but it sure doesn't seem like Austin. I look down at my watch and I am on track for a 11:59/mile pace. My
fellow joggers seem to be imbibing tallboys of hazy beers. No one has thrown up yet.

I meet a guy somewhere near what I think is the end of the race. We have been running for well past 2 hours. No one else is on the course,
and the tree lined boulevard has given way to some desolate patchy shrubland. My new friend and I run into the Austin airport, essentially
into the international arrivals section. The security point us to the main check in terminal, where we ask how much more of the race is
there to go. The check in agents tell us to go back to the baggage claim to get a new wristband. I wake up.
