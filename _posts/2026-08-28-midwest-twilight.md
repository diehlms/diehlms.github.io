It was a beatiful blue and gold twilight somewhere in the midwest. The sun had just sagged beneath the horizon, it's last remaining rays bouncing off some inexplicably tall skyscrapers adorning the fields nearby. Inexplicable because I am definitely in some kind of pasture or field. Somewhere nearby someone is growing something. The grass is tennis ball green and thick. I walk along a bike path saddling a ridgeline I find myself on due to something resembling a promotional video beamed into my brain; on the video screen is a nice seeming couple, like a mommy daddy vlogging duo. There is a polite, inoffensive charm to them. Very midwest.
