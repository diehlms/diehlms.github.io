Was on a drive east with N*. We were rolling around banks of big deciduous trees, standing tall and slanting slightly over the highway bends. The sky was steel grey and full, the clouds straining like a pillowcase filled with water about to rip through the cloth. We had a nice convo about this and that. Could not for the life of me remember what any topic was. If memory serves we were deep in tennessee, ripping up through stubborn apalachia valley land.

We had to stop for the night. Some tall quarry tower served as the rendezvous point for some union outfit. These workers were all women in overalls, grease stained and muddy. No idea how but we two boys had infiltrated their session and were takin is as equals. We laughed, though did not ask what they were up to. There was a jolly spirit in the air.
