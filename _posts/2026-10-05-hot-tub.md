At some point of some kind of bachelor or birthday party, I go to take a splash in the hot tub. We were in what could be mistaken for one of those Costa Rican cloud jungle bungalos. It was a fairly tame party all things considered. In fact, it may have even been my bachelor party, in that everyone was there to celebrate me. But the faces and people felt unfamiliar to me, like it was week two of freshman year of college; I know who you are, but I don't know you in any real sense.

I was feeling ill, or maybe just slightly nauteous. I guess we had been drinking quite a bit. I sit down in the hot tub I guess to relax and help my body shed some toxins. Well shed it did, as I look down and some yellowish brown detritus clouds the bottom of the hot tub. Should I also mention that everyone was in the hot tub. Well they were. I notice the cloud immediately, and try to conceal it by sitting on it using some maneuver to inflate my swim trunks so as to trap the substance the way fireman throw a blanket over a small inferno.

Everyone who noticed it was kind enough not to yelp or draw attention to it. Slowly one-by-one the participants of the hot tub session evacuated to their corridors for what I presume to take a shower. I remain in the hot tub, not willing to unstake my tent so to speak and let out the embarresing evidence of my body breaking down.
