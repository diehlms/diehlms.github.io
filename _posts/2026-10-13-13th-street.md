It was the end of a relationship, but with M* not C*. Summertime in Austin as we drove through town with the car glass radiating heat shimmers. I had dropped her off I guess as I was soon driving alone. I was caught up in thought of "what am I going to do now". I thought of what people would think, but I was mostly caught up in the crash of loneliness soon to come as I would have to, sooner or later, face a night in with no one around. No one to bounce ideas off of, no one to catch my porous mind. No one to listen to about the triviality of day to day living.

I drove down 13th st at midday. The world was completely washed out and a sickly grey yellow tone.
