I had a great one the other night. It was so good I woke up right afterwards, around 3am, and told myself to jot down the notes in my phone. Alas, I continued to sleep and after waking up from my alarm, making coffee, and feeding scooty nearly everything I dreamt of had vanished from memory.

From what I remember, I certainly was at a snack shack with my friend JJ. A snack shack is, from what I remember of days in the Rio Grande Valley, a palace of junk food and sugary drinks. I really don't think I've ever set foot in one, so this is all from vibe and predjudice.

We sat in the snack shack, having a nice time chatting and shooting the breeze. And that's it. Again, there was a lot more that happened afterwards. I can picture the sky and the ribbons of blue, red, and orange stretched taught towards the western horizon. But yeah, that's all I got today.
